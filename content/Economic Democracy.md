[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_democracy)

We've concluded that government ought to be controlled democratically, but bottom-up control of the economy is limited to the "power of the purse" and exit.  This is described particularly well by [[Elizabeth Anderson's Private Government]].

The concept of economic democracy advocates for democratic control of society's productive capacity.  In my view, this looks like [[cooperative|cooperatives]], [[social ownership]], etc. as opposed to private equity purchasing the stores and apartments on your town's main street.

This somewhat ties into my writing on [Inflationary Equity](https://writing.drewmca.net/3lvhavefoac2b), which advocates for continuously awarding shares of a corporation to workers as they contribute toward its production.
