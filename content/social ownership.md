[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_ownership)

An easy definition in my mind is a relationship where the owner of some property is involved in that property.  A house you live in, a business you work at, a car you drive.  This exists in opposition to private ownership, exemplified by private equity: ownership in name only, disconnected from the thing and everything around it, solely for the purposes of profit.

Private ownership opens the relationship up to externalities, whereas social ownership tends to internalize them due to proximity.
